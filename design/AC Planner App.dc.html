2Ritualist — full screen set, built on 1a

Ritualist. Card-stack home, every module reorderable or hideable. Reminders push the day’s mantra and open tasks so the app finds you, not the other way round. Theme follows the phone and can be overridden in settings — dark set below, warm-cream light set after it. Sign-in is email/password (your existing Supabase), Google, or phone OTP, with biometric unlock and a separate PIN on the journal. 14-day trial, then Pro. A returning user always lands straight on today: the quiz runs once and the app never asks again.
