RITUALIST
Privacy policy

Your entries never leave your phone.

Ritualist has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you write lives in your device's own storage. We cannot read it, because it never reaches us.

Last updated 23 August 2026 · Applies to Ritualist for Android, version 0.1 onwards

What the app stores

On your device only, Ritualist keeps the priorities you write for each day, the habits you create and the dates you marked them done, your reminder times, and your light-or-dark preference.

That data is held in the app's private storage, which other apps on your phone cannot read. Uninstalling Ritualist deletes all of it.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, so we do not have your name, email address or phone number. The app makes no requests to any A&C Creative Company server, sends no crash reports, and contains no advertising or tracking software.

The one exception is fonts: the first time you open the app it downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts and caches them on the device. That request reaches Google, not us, and carries no information about you or your entries. Google's handling of it is covered by their own privacy policy.

Permissions the app asks for

Notifications
Used to send your daily reminders. Asked for when you switch reminders on, not at launch. Decline it and the rest of the app still works.
Run after restart
Android forgets scheduled reminders when the phone restarts. This permission lets the app quietly put them back. It does nothing else.

Backups you make yourself

Settings offers a backup that copies your data to your clipboard as plain text, so you can paste it wherever you like. Once it leaves the app it is yours to look after: whatever you paste it into — a note, an email, a message — is governed by that service's privacy policy, not ours. We never see it.

Children

Ritualist is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Where the app comes from

Ritualist is currently distributed as a downloadable file from our GitHub releases page rather than an app store. Only install it from a link published by A&C Creative Company. A copy obtained anywhere else may have been altered, and this policy would not describe what it does.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds accounts, cloud sync or payments, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the release notes will say so plainly.
